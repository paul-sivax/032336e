SIVAX Agent connects AI agents to paid product discovery and verifiable XRPL order receipts.

- **A2A:** Agent Card discovery and structured tasks let other AI agents delegate product search and order requests to SIVAX under human spending and purchase approvals.
- **x402:** HTTP 402 challenges let agents pay for RFQ and order APIs with native XRP within approved budgets. RFQ fees settle once per batch, enabling automated pay-per-use access.
- **MPT:** One receipt per approved simulated order (AssetScale=0, MaximumAmount=1) binds an auditable XRPL record to encrypted product terms. Issuance, holder authorization and delivery use sponsored custody.
- **Private terms:** AES-256-GCM metadata and authenticated server-side decryption let owners view terms without exposing plaintext on-chain.

Quotes are precomputed; investment principal is simulated. In Testnet mode, XRP fee settlement and MPT issuance, delivery and cleanup are real ledger transactions.
